Hi and thanks for making use of this pixel art asset pack! A few key points to note when using the sprites/spritesheets:
 - for the 16x16 sprites, some animations (Jump and Walk) do not fit fully on 16x16, so the spritesheets are adjusted to fit
 - 32x32 sprites are all centered and all animations fit evenly within 32x32
 - If you have any questions or comments, let me know via a comment on my itch.io page
 - Enjoy these sprites, and share what you make with me, it makes me happy!
